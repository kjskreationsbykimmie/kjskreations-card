KJ's Kreations by Kimmie — Virtual Business Card

Files:
- index.html — mobile-friendly virtual business card
- logo.png — supplied KJ's Kreations logo
- kimmie-jones.vcf — downloadable contact card

To make the QR permanent/dynamic:
1. Host this folder at a permanent URL you control.
2. Create the QR code for that URL.
3. In the future, update/replace index.html at the SAME URL. The printed QR remains unchanged.

Important: Do not print a QR code until the final permanent URL has been chosen.
